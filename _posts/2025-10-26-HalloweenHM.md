Two weeks after Krakow I am doing another Half Marathon, this time in Worksop, one hour from Sheffield. I have never been here so it is a nice way to see a new place! This one is a bit more hilly compared to Krakow with 220m of ascent. It passes through Clumber park and the colors of autumn are really beautiful. Everything is on road. I finished it in 1h46 which is my second HM best time. Ranking was 355/1208.
