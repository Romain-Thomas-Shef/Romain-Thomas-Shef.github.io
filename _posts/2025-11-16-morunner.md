Last preparation race before the SaintLyon! This one is the MoRunner Half Marathon at Heaton Park in Manchester. It is made in support of Prostate cancer (hence the name). It is another looped race, with 4 loops of ~5k. There is a descent climb and the park is beautiful. Also it starts to be a bit cold! The race went fine and I finished it in 1h46 for a ranking of 35/213!
