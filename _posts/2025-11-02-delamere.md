Here we go again after the halloween Marathon of last week! This time it is a trail Marathon in the Delamere forest. It is a bit far from my place so I am sleeping close to the race. I still managed to arrive 2min late for the race though! This is the first time I do a looped race. This one is four times 10.5k. Each loop start with a big climb and then wanders down to the forest. The colors were beautiful! The goal was not to push the speed but just to do the km so it was an easy pace. I did the 42k amnd 715m of climb in 4h26 which got me the 65 place in the ranking (out of 182).
