Let's continue on the preparation of the Saintelyon with the Half Marathon of Krakow! The day was perfect for running, cloudy and not warm at all. It is a very nice race starting in the Tauron Arena and passing through the city center and along the river. Really enjoyed it! I finished it in 1:45: which is my half marathon PB! Ranking is 3898/14185 so that was quite a good race!
