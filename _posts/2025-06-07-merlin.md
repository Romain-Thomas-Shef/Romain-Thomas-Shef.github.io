To finish on the preparation of the The wall which is just one  weeks later I am running the Mavericks Peak Merlin 28k. It is a really nice run in the peak district and start 40 minutes from Sheffield. It has a very nice route passing through forest and a bit of climb (~580m). I did the race in 2h51 and finished 127/415. It was a really good race! Will do it again!
